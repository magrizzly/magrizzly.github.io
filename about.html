About & Tech Stack

Software engineering ethos, technical toolkit, and privacy-respecting architectural principles behind the magrizzly hub.

Who is magrizzly?

I am a software engineer, tinkerer, and open-source enthusiast passionate about high-efficiency systems, clean user interfaces, and preserving digital autonomy.

This website serves as my public home base on the open web. It contains interactive utilities, software projects, and quick reference materials without the tracking scripts, heavy client bundles, or invasive data harvesting that dominate modern web properties.

Core Principles

  • Privacy First: No analytics trackers, no telemetry pixels, and no ad beacons. Client tools run directly inside your browser.
  • Minimalism & Speed: Prefer zero-build vanilla standards over cumbersome framework bloat whenever appropriate.
  • Open Source & Reproducibility: All code hosted here is open for review, auditing, and learning.
  • Long-Term Durability: Standard HTML/CSS never goes out of fashion or breaks due to abandoned npm packages.
