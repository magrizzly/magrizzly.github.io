Quick Setup Connection & Playback Recommendations

Connection & Playback Guide

Follow these three simple best practices to ensure optimal streaming speeds and high visual fidelity anywhere in the world.

STEP 01

Remote Access

When outside the home network, make sure you enter the full secure URL including https://:

https://mageemedia.tailcc77cc.ts.net

Tailscale mesh encryption ensures direct, secure, end-to-end encrypted streams with zero public internet exposure.

STEP 02

Video Playback Quality

During video playback in Jellyfin or Swiftfin, tap the Gear icon.

If streaming on slower mobile data, switch Quality from Auto to 1080p - 10 Mbps or 720p - 4 Mbps to prevent stuttering.

STEP 03

Audio & Subtitles

Jellyfin supports full subtitle selection (including forced subtitles for foreign dialogue) and multiple audio tracks (e.g. Japanese audio + English subtitles for anime).

Switch tracks instantly right within the video playback controls.

Troubleshooting Common Questions & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about accounts, remote playback, offline downloads, and streaming hardware.

I see a "Cannot connect to server" error. What should I check?

Verify the server URL has no spaces or typos: https://mageemedia.tailcc77cc.ts.net. Be sure to include the full prefix https://.

If accessing over mobile data or external Wi-Fi, ensure your device can reach the server endpoint or that your Tailscale connection is active.

How do I get an account?

Contact Charlie directly with your preferred username. An account will be provisioned giving you seamless unified access across Jellyfin, Jellyseerr, and Immich.

For Plex, provide your registered Plex account email so an invite to the Magee Plex library can be sent.

Video is buffering or stuttering on iPhone or Apple TV?

Download Swiftfin from the App Store instead of the default Jellyfin mobile app.

Swiftfin is built specifically for Apple platforms and uses native Apple AVPlayer and VLC rendering engines for buttery-smooth hardware-accelerated 4K HDR playback and subtitle rendering.

Can I download movies to watch offline during flights or road trips?

Yes! The official Jellyfin mobile app supports offline syncing for movies and TV episodes directly to your smartphone or tablet storage.

Simply navigate to any movie or episode, tap the three dots icon (⋮), and select Download while connected to Wi-Fi before traveling.

How do media requests work in Jellyseerr?

Log into Jellyseerr with your Jellyfin credentials. Search for any released or upcoming movie or show and click Request.

The server automates discovery, downloading, and sorting into the appropriate library. Once processed, it will immediately appear on your Jellyfin home screen.
